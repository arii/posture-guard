Camera placement

  • Person fully in frame, facing the camera
  • Mount the camera HIGH — on a shelf or wall mount roughly 0.4–0.5 m above head height — and tilt it down about 45° at your mid-torso. At the normal ~0.6–0.8 m working distance this fits head and hips in frame
  • Alternative for deep desks: sit 1.0–1.5 m away with the camera at chest/neck height aimed at mid-torso
  • USB 3 port for stable depth
